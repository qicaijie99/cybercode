# Provider brand icons

These assets identify third-party model providers inside CyberCode. Product names and
marks remain the property of their respective owners.

- Most SVG assets come from `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.94.0, published by
  [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) under the MIT License.
- OAuth product marks use their matching Lobe Icons or Simple Icons brand assets
  and are bundled locally for reliable rendering in the desktop app.
- `reka.png`, `openvecta.svg`, `pioneer.svg`, `bytez.svg`, and the bundled
  aggregator/gateway marks come from
  [OmniRoute](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute), also under the MIT License.

The assets are bundled locally so provider identity remains correct offline and under
the desktop app's content-security policy.
